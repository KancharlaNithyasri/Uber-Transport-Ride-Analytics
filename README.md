# Uber-Transport-Ride-Analytics
# 🚖 Uber Transport & Ride Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI analytics solution designed to analyze ride-hailing operations, track financial performance, assess customer and driver satisfaction, and identify cancellation trends to optimize demand forecasting.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project processes and visualizes comprehensive trip data to help stakeholders understand fleet efficiency, revenue streams, and lost opportunity drivers. By analyzing over **93,000 completed bookings** alongside cancellation dynamics, the dashboard delivers actionable insights to improve service reliability and maximize revenue.

---

## 📊 Key Metrics & Highlights

* **Total Completed Bookings:** `93,000+` trips analyzed across multiple service tiers.
* **Total Revenue:** `$52 Million` tracked and segmented by payment types and ride categories.
* **Cancellation Analysis:** Examined **57,000 lost bookings** (driver and customer cancellations) to reduce drop-offs and improve match rates.
* **Satisfaction Metrics:** Integrated dual-rating models evaluating both Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) and Driver Satisfaction scores.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

* **Business Intelligence:** Power BI Desktop
* **Data Transformation:** Power Query (ETL pipeline setup, data cleaning, and column conditioning)
* **Calculations:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for calculated measures, KPIs, time intelligence, and dynamic filters

---

## 🔑 Key Features & Insights

### 1. Executive Performance Overview
* Track primary KPIs: Total Revenue, Completed Rides, Cancellation Rate, and Average Fare.
* High-level temporal analysis showing booking trends across peak hours, weekdays, and weekends.

### 2. Revenue & Financial Analytics
* Revenue distribution across payment methods (Cash, Credit Card, In-App Wallet).
* Fare breakdown by distance, vehicle type (Economy, Premium, XL), and trip distance brackets.

### 3. Customer & Driver Experience
* Rating distribution and feedback analysis across driver and rider segments.
* Correlation between wait times, trip duration, and overall ratings.

### 4. Cancellation & Loss Diagnostics
* Deep dive into **57,000 cancelled bookings** categorized by cancellation reason (e.g., long wait times, driver cancellation, rider change of mind).
* Geographic and time-of-day mapping of high-cancellation hotspots to assist with dynamic supply-demand forecasting.
